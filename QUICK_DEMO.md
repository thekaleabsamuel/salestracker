# 🚀 Quick Demo: Business Selection & Tracking

## ✨ **What's New - Checkboxes & Add Button!**

Your search results now have **checkboxes** and an **"Add to Tracking"** button right in the search table!

## 🎯 **How to Use (Step by Step)**

### **Step 1: Search for Businesses**
1. Enter: **"IT companies"** + **"Denver, CO"**
2. Click **Search**
3. See results in the **Search Results** tab

### **Step 2: Select Businesses to Track**
1. **Check the boxes** next to businesses you want to track
2. You can select **one, multiple, or all** businesses
3. Use the **header checkbox** to select all results
4. See selection count in the **"Add X to Tracking"** button

### **Step 3: Add to Tracking**
1. Click the **"Add X to Tracking"** button
2. Businesses automatically get added with:
   - Contact Status: "Not Contacted"
   - Priority: "Medium"
   - Added Date: Today
3. **Automatically switches** to the **Business Tracking** tab

### **Step 4: Manage Your Pipeline**
1. **Edit contact status** (click edit icon)
2. **Add notes** about conversations
3. **Set follow-up dates**
4. **Update priority levels**
5. **Export** your complete pipeline

## 🔍 **Visual Features**

### **Selection Indicators**
- ✅ **Individual checkboxes** for each business
- ✅ **Header checkbox** to select all results
- ✅ **Selection count** in the Add button
- ✅ **Info alert** showing how many selected

### **Smart Button States**
- **Disabled** when no businesses selected
- **Dynamic text** showing count: "Add 5 to Tracking"
- **Primary color** to make it stand out

1. **Search** → Find IT companies, offices, schools in Colorado
