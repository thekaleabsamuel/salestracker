# Business Tracking & Spreadsheet Feature Demo

## 🎯 **What's New**

Your Sales Tracker now includes a powerful business tracking system that works like an Excel spreadsheet! Here's how it works:

### ✨ **Key Features**

1. **Business Selection**: Click checkboxes to select multiple businesses
2. **Add to Spreadsheet**: Bulk add selected businesses to your tracking grid
3. **Sales Pipeline Management**: Track status, priority, notes, and follow-ups
4. **Editable Grid**: Click to edit any field directly in the table
5. **Export to CSV**: Download your tracking data for external use
6. **Persistent Storage**: All data is saved locally in your browser

## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **Step 1: Search for Businesses**
1. Go to the **Search** tab
2. Enter business type (e.g., "Mexican restaurants")
3. Set location and radius
4. Click **Search**

### **Step 2: Select Businesses**
1. **Individual Selection**: Click the checkbox next to each business you want to track
2. **Bulk Selection**: Use the header checkbox to select/deselect all
3. **Quick Add**: Click the **+** icon in the Actions column to add individual businesses

### **Step 3: Add to Spreadsheet**
1. After selecting businesses, you'll see a blue selection bar
2. Click **"Add to Spreadsheet"** button
3. You'll automatically be taken to the **Tracking Grid** tab

### **Step 4: Manage Your Pipeline**
1. **Edit Status**: Click the status chip to change from dropdown
2. **Set Priority**: Choose Low, Medium, High, or Urgent
3. **Add Notes**: Click notes field to add call details, objections, etc.
4. **Set Follow-up Dates**: Schedule when to call back
5. **Track Contacts**: Record who you spoke with and when

## 📊 **Tracking Grid Columns**

### **Business Info**
- Business name, address, phone, website
- All original search data is preserved

### **Status Tracking**
- **Not Contacted** - New leads
- **Called - No Answer** - Left voicemail
- **Called - Spoke to Someone** - Had conversation
- **Follow-up Required** - Need to call back
- **Not Interested** - Qualified out
- **Interested - Follow Up** - Hot lead!
- **Qualified Lead** - Ready for proposal
- **Converted** - Closed deal!
- **Lost** - Lost to competition

### **Priority Levels**
- **Low** - Green chip
- **Medium** - Yellow chip  
- **High** - Red chip
- **Urgent** - Red chip

### **Custom Fields**
- **Notes** - Call details, objections, next steps
- **Follow-up Date** - When to call back
- **Contact Person** - Who you spoke with
- **Last Contact Date** - When you last called
- **Last Updated** - When you last edited
